# Scan depth sensitivity of the native Jass disagreements

Scan 3.1 rescored all 190 legal successors on the 20 positions
where LCB and native Jass chose different moves at 50,000 nodes.
This is a descriptive check on the selected disagreements.
The two Jass choices per position come from the published searches.
All 20 paired regrets were ordinary at both reference depths.

| Scan depth | Optima LCB / native | Lower / equal / higher LCB regret | Mean LCB minus native regret (centimen) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 16 | 3 / 5 | 10 / 2 / 8 | +11.80 |
| 18 | 7 / 4 | 12 / 1 / 7 | +11.65 |

Three positions reversed which arm had lower regret and one tie
became a 6-centimen advantage for LCB. The two largest depth-16
costs persisted. On `B:WK3,16,19,24:B15,37`, the regret of
LCB's 37-41 against native's 37-42 rose from +243 to +291
centimen. The second-largest cost changed from +51 to +50.
Native selected a Scan optimum at both depths in both cases.
Omitting the +291 case gives a descriptive mean of −3.05 centimen
on 19 positions. The study's primary reference stays at depth 16.
