# Five-arena spatial Jass replication

The five independent Lidraughts arenas contributed 134,670 eligible
positions, including 473 with both L and LCB matches in PC Blues.
LCB changed the initial top-three ordering on 64 positions, 55 of
them from CARZrQpl. All 4,257 Jass searches reached their requested
node budgets. The archived traces and paired receipts passed the
pooled validation.

## Primary comparison at 50,000 nodes

Scan 3.1 evaluated all legal target successors at depth 16.
LCB and the exact-legal-actions control L each chose a Scan optimum
on 153 of 473 positions. They both hit on 152, each hit alone on
one, and neither hit on 319. The mean paired regret difference
(LCB minus L) was **+0.034 centimen** on 469 ordinary pairs from
336 owning games in five arenas. The hierarchical 95% bootstrap
interval was **[−0.021, +0.116] centimen**. Four pairs were loss
to loss. The reference scores show no advantage for LCB on this
cohort.

LCB changed the selected move on five of the 64 positions with
a different initial order. The signed costs were −6, −3, +4, +10
and +11 centimen. Positive values mean a higher regret for LCB.
There were no category changes. The ordinary mean on the 64
positions was +0.250 centimen [−0.145, +1.143]. The full-cohort
estimate remained between +0.028 and +0.039 after omitting each
arena in turn. Omitting CARZrQpl gave +0.032 on 125 ordinary pairs.
