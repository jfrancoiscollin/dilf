# Scan target-depth sensitivity — 12 November 2026

The [freeze](https://github.com/jfrancoiscollin/dilf/commit/66aee56a6f73f096c1cbcc820f1dd69a022a8504)
selected 39 distinct positions across 34 owning games: the union of
36 matches recovered by counting movers (LCB versus exact occupancy
with blockers, LMB) and 13 targets where LCB selected a different
source from L (exact legal actions). Ten positions belong to both
subsets. This is a descriptive check on a selected subset of the three
holdouts. Source rankings, including tie-breaks, were fixed at Scan
successor depth 12. The target reference alone moved from depth 16 to
18, using the same Scan 3.1 executable, one thread, a cold table, and
no book or tablebases.

## Results

| Positions | Depth | LCB first choice optimal | LCB top three contain an optimum | LCB mean best-of-three regret | L mean best-of-three regret |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| All 39 | 16 | 6 | 17 | 4.15 | 4.56 |
| All 39 | 18 | 5 | 19 | 3.77 | 4.21 |
| 36 recovered | 16 | 5 | 16 | 4.39 | 4.86 |
| 36 recovered | 18 | 5 | 17 | 4.03 | 4.50 |
| 13 with changed source | 16 | 2 | 5 | 1.92 | 3.15 |
| 13 with changed source | 18 | 2 | 8 | 1.31 | 2.62 |

Regret is in centimen and includes exact hits. All positions had
ordinary scores. Depth 18 had the same set of optimal moves on 28 of
39 targets and disjoint optimum sets on seven. On the 36 recovered
positions, LCB's best-of-three regret averaged 7.63 centimen over
the 19 depth-18 misses, with a maximum of 37. Its first-choice regret
averaged 31.65 over 31 misses, with a maximum of 313. On those same
36 positions at depth 18, L found an optimum with its first choice
in 4 cases and in its top three in 16.

The small paired best-of-three difference persisted across depths:
LCB minus L was −0.41 and −0.44 centimen over all 39 targets, and
−1.23 and −1.31 on the 13 changed-source targets. At depth 18, LCB
reduced best-of-three regret in four of those 13 cases, tied in six,
and increased it in three. These results support low regret for the
three-move lists on these targets despite changes in which moves
Scan identifies as optimal. They leave the benefit for Jass search
at fixed node budgets open.

## Reproduction and next control

The [manifest](manifest.json) reproduced byte for byte before scoring.
The pinned Scan build scored all 364 legal successors in 3 min 12 s
on one remote thread. The [target receipt](target.json) and
[analysis](summary.json) are archived. The analysis reproduced byte for
byte with Python hash seeds 7 and 61. It also reproduced every selected
depth-16 outcome from the archived scores and rejected a changed
receipt depth and a missing action. The 116 MTDP tests (including the
pinned Jass catalogue), 643 DILF tests and 26-game PC Blues replay
passed.

From the DILF root, with the pinned adapter and Scan paths from the
[replication procedure](../../README.md#score-once-per-distinct-fen-and-depth):

```sh
study=research/mtdp/jass_benchmark/eighth-replication/scan-results
export PYTHONPATH="$PWD:$DRAUGHT_MASTER_ROOT"
python "$study/depth18/sensitivity.py" --index "$study/packet-index.json" \
  --manifest /tmp/mtdp-depth18-manifest.json
cmp "$study/depth18/manifest.json" /tmp/mtdp-depth18-manifest.json
python -m research.mtdp.jass_scan_reference --role target --depth 18 \
  --manifest "$study/depth18/manifest.json" --scan "$SCAN_ROOT/scan_linux" \
  --output /tmp/mtdp-depth18-target.json
cmp "$study/depth18/target.json" /tmp/mtdp-depth18-target.json
python "$study/depth18/sensitivity.py" --index "$study/packet-index.json" \
  --manifest "$study/depth18/manifest.json" --reference /tmp/mtdp-depth18-target.json \
  --output /tmp/mtdp-depth18-summary.json
cmp "$study/depth18/summary.json" /tmp/mtdp-depth18-summary.json
```
