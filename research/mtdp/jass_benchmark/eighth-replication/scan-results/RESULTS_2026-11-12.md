# Three-event MTDP replication — 12 November 2026

Three independent 5+0 FMJD tournaments contributed 29,537 eligible
quiet mover-men-only positions. The 505 public game IDs in the exports
and all eligible FENs were disjoint across tournaments. PC Blues
provided the transfer sources. Scan 3.1 ranked source actions at
successor depth 12 and evaluated targets at successor depth 16.

| Matching rule | Matched / eligible | Scan optimum in first choice | Scan optimum in top three | Mean first-choice regret | Mean best-of-three regret |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| L, exact legal actions | 326 / 29,537 | 82 / 326 | 202 / 326 | 42.91 | 8.19 |
| LM, exact mover occupancy | 102 / 29,537 | 39 / 102 | 77 / 102 | 28.15 | 1.69 |
| LCB, mover count and exact immediate blockers | 138 / 29,537 | 41 / 138 | 93 / 138 | 28.16 | 2.40 |

Regret is in centimen (100 centimen per man). The means include zero
when the candidate is optimal and exclude the one non-ordinary position
per arm. The rows describe different matched populations. Comparisons
of quality use the same target positions below. Under LCB the source
and target optimum sets were disjoint in 89 of 138 matches. First-choice
regret reached 0.5 man in 15 matches and 6.26 men in the worst case.
Best-of-three regret never reached 0.5 man under LCB (maximum 0.42).

## Preregistered paired comparison

On the 138 positions matched by both L and LCB, spanning 98 owning
games, LCB found a Scan optimum in its first choice 41 times versus 43
for L, and in its top three 93 times versus 94 for L. The primary
mean best-of-three regret difference (LCB minus L) was **−0.12 centimen**
on 137 ordinary pairs (95% tournament/game bootstrap interval
**[−0.52, +0.19]**). The three tournament means ranged from −0.21 to
−0.04 centimen. The first-choice difference, a supplementary measure,
was −2.72 centimen [−9.09, +0.05].

LCB changed the selected source in 13 of the 138 positions, each from
a different game. For best of three, it reduced regret in 5, increased
it in 6 and tied in 2 (mean −1.23 centimen, interval [−5.55, +2.18]).
For first choice the mean change was −28.62 centimen, with the largest
single reduction contributing 256 of the 372 centimen of net reduction.
Removing that observation as a descriptive sensitivity gives −9.67
centimen. The subset is small and conditional on the source changing.
On the 102 common LMB/LCB positions, best-of-three regret was identical.

## Positions recovered by counting pions

Relaxing exact mover occupancy to count while retaining blockers added
36 matches in 32 owning games, a 35.3% gain over LMB. On those same 36
positions, LCB preserved a Scan optimum with its first choice in 5
cases (L: 7) and in its top three in 16 cases (L: 17). LCB's mean
best-of-three regret was 4.39 centimen including exact hits, and 7.90
centimen across the 20 misses (maximum 42). Its first-choice mean
across the 31 misses was 32.29 centimen (maximum 305).

These results support LCB as a way to recover additional low-regret
three-move lists under this Scan reference. Exact first-choice transfer
was infrequent and the paired comparison did not establish an advantage
over exact legal actions alone. The study measured offline transfer
quality. A claim about Jass playing strength requires a node-matched
engine experiment.

## Reproduction and follow-up

The [freeze commit](https://github.com/jfrancoiscollin/dilf/commit/29918717542b7162a62be1042fefe4f8e09085c4)
preceded all new scores. The [scoring procedure](../README.md#score-once-per-distinct-fen-and-depth)
deduplicated 144 source FENs (1,404 actions) and 326 target FENs (3,156
actions). Each Scan process used one thread, a cold transposition
table, and no book or tablebases. Jass and the pinned FMJD adapter
matched all 4,560 legal actions exactly. All 116 MTDP tests, 643 DILF
tests and the 26-game PC Blues replay passed.
