# Sixth holdout — geometry versus exact legal actions

The [preregistered ablation](../SIXTH_GEOMETRY_ABLATION_PROTOCOL_2026-11-12.md)
compares exact legal actions alone (L) with exact legal actions plus
the quiet-man signature (LG). The independent 5+0 tournament and
matching rules were [frozen before scoring](https://github.com/jfrancoiscollin/dilf/commit/e0ea77201179bbd8fe03c3a26d130bf6e90a37d1).
The pinned Scan 3.1 binary evaluated 722 legal successors of 76 source
positions at depth 12, and 1,306 legal successors of 138 targets at
depth 16. Both references use one thread, cold TT, no book or tablebases.
All selected positions and legal scores were retained.

## Match coverage

| Arm | Matched among 8,633 eligible targets | Unmatched | Mean matching source FENs per matched target |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| L | 138 (1.60%) | 8,495 | 1.51 |
| LG | 39 (0.45%) | 8,594 | 1.26 |

The geometry arm retains 39/138 (28.3%) of the legal matches. Among
the 99 targets matched only by L, the selected source always had
a different set of mover-man squares. In 90/99 it had the same
geometric blocker set. This post-freeze structural description uses
the selected legal-only source, independently of Scan's scores.

## Conditional transfer quality

regret figures are in men on Scan's scale.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| L | 87/138 | 38/138 | 101/138 |

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| L, first | 136 | 0.244 / 0.04 / 5.22 | 16 | 2 ordinary → loss |

| LG, first | 39 | 0.139 / 0.03 / 2.56 | 2 | 0 |
| LG, best of three | 39 | 0.004 / 0.00 / 0.09 | 0 | 0 |

The extra legal-only matches cover a target optimum with the three
moves in 66/99 cases. The apparent quality difference between
conditional cohorts combines source selection with differences in
which targets matched. Unmatched positions have no regret assigned.

## Paired comparison on the 39 common targets

The two arms select the same source on 38/39 targets. In the only
changed match, the first transferred move's regret fell from 0.31
to zero. This increases first-choice coverage from 12/39 to 13/39,
a paired difference of +2.6 percentage points (95% interval 0 to
+7.7). The mean first-choice regret difference (LG minus L) is
−0.008 men (95% interval −0.024 to 0.000).

Both lists cover an optimum in 35/39 cases and the best of the
three has identical regret on **every paired target**. The
observed contrast and its bootstrap interval are both zero.
The 39 targets are assigned to 26 games, with 10,000 whole-game
resamples, seed 20261112. Removing three targets seen in multiple
games leaves 36 targets in 25 games and the same identical
top-three outcomes. The paired estimate depends on this selection
rule and one event. It gives no estimate of how geometry would
behave with a different source selection policy.

## Reproduction

Start with the [published freeze](FREEZE.md) and
[engine digests](provenance.json). Use the pinned draught-master FMJD
adapter and the pinned Scan 3.1 binary. Run from DILF's root.

```sh
set -eu
export PYTHONPATH="$PWD:$DRAUGHT_MASTER_ROOT"
study=research/mtdp/jass_benchmark/sixth-xmzj1bLG
tmpdir=$(mktemp -d)
gzip -dc "$study/score-plan.json.gz" > "$tmpdir/score-plan.json"
for role in source target; do
  case "$role" in source) depth=12 ;; target) depth=16 ;; esac
  python -m research.mtdp.jass_scan_reference --role "$role" --depth "$depth" \
    --manifest "$tmpdir/score-plan.json" --scan "$SCAN_ROOT/scan_linux" \
    --output "$tmpdir/$role.json"
  gzip -dc "$study/$role-depth$depth.json.gz" | cmp - "$tmpdir/$role.json"
done
python -m research.mtdp.jass_benchmark.geometry_ablation analyze \
  --provenance "$study/provenance.json" --source "$tmpdir/source.json" \
  --target "$tmpdir/target.json" --output "$tmpdir/comparison.json"
gzip -dc "$study/comparison.json.gz" | cmp - "$tmpdir/comparison.json"
```

The archive records both matching catalogues for every eligible
target. The comparison retains ordinary and categorical outcomes,
source multiplicity histograms, the matched records and the paired
game-cluster intervals. The scoring plan contains all selected
sources and all targets matched by either arm.

Two independent scores for each role reproduced byte for byte. Source
scores also agree across 77 shared source-reference instances with
the five preceding cohorts. Two analyses with distinct hash seeds
produced SHA-256
`91da053cb0c048f4ef3563a321d914eb8d59772ce21cdf9d9e4b038921e5f397`.
102 MTDP tests and 643 DILF tests passed. The optional live Jass
catalogue test was skipped because its binary was absent. This
experiment uses canonical DILF actions and no Jass search.
