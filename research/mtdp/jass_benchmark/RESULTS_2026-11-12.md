# Jass move ordering on an independent rapid cohort

The [frozen cohort](README.md) contains 28 target positions from 17
Lidraughts games. Each target has a PC Blues source with the same quiet-man
signature and identical canonical legal actions. The cohort was committed
before scoring. Positions within a game and positions sharing a source
can be related.

## Scan 3.1 reference

At fixed successor depth 12, **18 of 28 pairs have disjoint optimal sets**.
The canonical best source move is also optimal on 10 targets. The maximum
regret from that move is **1.34 men** on the Scan scale. A transferred list
of three contains a Scan optimum on 18 targets, and the maximum regret
of its best available candidate is **0.12 men**. No list of three spans
the complete legal set. The [reference scores](scan-reference.json) cover
all legal actions on the 28 targets and 24 distinct sources.

## Paired Jass searches

Each of the 252 searches consumed exactly its assigned budget. The two
ordered arms moved up to three candidates to the front and kept all
other legal actions in generated order. The traces verify the injected
order and depth-one coverage. Jass started each search with a fresh table
and the board and side to move from FEN, with no prior game history.

Deltas below are the regret of the selected Jass move with transfer minus
its regret in the comparison arm. Negative values favor transfer.
All 28 pairs in each comparison have ordinary Scan scores.

| Nodes | Mean delta vs canonical (men) | Better / worse vs canonical | Mean delta vs native (men) | Better / worse vs native | Scan optima, transfer / canonical / native |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10,000 | −0.002 | 4 / 5 | +0.004 | 3 / 5 | 5 / 5 / 6 |
| 50,000 | −0.015 | 11 / 7 | −0.017 | 8 / 7 | 9 / 5 / 6 |
| 200,000 | −0.007 | 8 / 3 | −0.015 | 6 / 5 | 7 / 2 / 4 |

Median regret deltas are zero across all comparisons. At the deepest
completed depth shared by all three arms, the median node deltas against
native are +1,012, +4,783.5 and +30,449 at the three budgets, respectively.
Native Jass completes a higher mean depth at each budget. The modest
quality differences and the extra nodes at a shared depth do not establish
a search-efficiency or playing-strength gain. The comparison with native
includes replacing its evolving root order with a fixed schedule.

The [summary](summary.json) also groups results by selected PC Blues source
game. The [paired receipts](paired-results.jsonl.gz) and
[root traces](root-order-traces.jsonl.gz) are gzip-compressed JSON Lines.
They include PV, Jass scores, stop reasons, nodes, time, and the list of
visited root actions per attempt. The [build receipt](jass-build-manifest.json)
links the binary and inputs by SHA-256.

## Reproduce

Use the pinned inputs and build flags from the [cohort](README.md) and the
[Jass preflight](../jass_preflight/README.md), with GCC 14.2.0 and CMake
4.4.4 for the archived build. From the DILF checkout:

```sh
experiment=research/mtdp/jass_benchmark
cmake --build "$JASS_BUILD" --target mtdp_jass_benchmark -j 4
PYTHONPATH="$PWD:$DRAUGHT_MASTER_ROOT" python -m research.mtdp.jass_scan_reference \
  --manifest "$experiment/cohort-manifest.json" --scan "$SCAN_ROOT/scan_linux" \
  --output /tmp/mtdp-jass-scan-reference.json
cmp /tmp/mtdp-jass-scan-reference.json "$experiment/scan-reference.json"
PYTHONPATH="$PWD" python "$experiment/run.py" \
  --manifest "$experiment/cohort-manifest.json" \
  --scan-reference /tmp/mtdp-jass-scan-reference.json \
  --runner "$JASS_BUILD/mtdp_jass_benchmark" --output-dir /tmp/mtdp-jass-results
gzip -dc "$experiment/root-order-traces.jsonl.gz" |
  cmp - /tmp/mtdp-jass-results/root-order-traces.jsonl
```

Repeated Scan scores and root traces matched byte for byte. The paired
search receipts matched after excluding elapsed wall time. All 79 MTDP
tests, 643 general DILF tests, and the Jass suite passed.
