# Seventh holdout — exact mover squares versus count and blockers

The [primary protocol](../../SEVENTH_MOVER_OCCUPANCY_PROTOCOL_2026-11-12.md)
and [count-and-blockers addendum](../../SEVENTH_MOVER_OCCUPANCY_ADDENDUM_2026-11-12.md)
preceded event selection. The 5+0 arena was
[frozen before scoring](https://github.com/jfrancoiscollin/dilf/commit/827deec4677312ecf771966002a97aa95261a52c).
Scan 3.1 scored all 586 legal successors of 60 source FENs at depth 12
and all 760 legal successors of 78 target FENs at depth 16. Both roles
used one thread, a cold TT, no book and no tablebases. There were no
missing scores or post-freeze exclusions.

## Coverage and conditional quality

L requires the same turn and exact legal actions. LM adds exact mover
man squares. LCB adds exact blocker squares and equality of the count
of mover men. LMB adds the exact mover man and blocker squares.
All arms use the same quiet population.

LM and LMB had exactly the same source-candidate lists on all
eligible targets in this event. Relative to L, LM retained 21/78
matches and LCB retained 32/78. LCB recovered 11 matches over LMB,
of which eight covered an optimum with the three source-ranked
moves. Across the 32 LCB matches, the maximum best-of-three regret
was 0.04 men. Unmatched targets have no regret assigned.

## Paired results

On the 21 L/LM targets from 17 owning games, only one selected source
changed. The primary paired mean best-of-three regret difference
(LM minus L) was zero, with identical regret on every common target.
The 95% whole-game bootstrap interval is [0, 0], reflecting the
identical observed regrets. First-choice regret and optimal coverage
also match exactly. LMB gives the same result.

On the 21 LCB/LMB targets, the selected source and both regrets are
identical. On the 32 LCB/L targets from 22 owning games, five sources
change. The paired LCB-minus-L mean best-of-three regret is +0.0013
men (95% interval −0.0031 to +0.0059) and the optimum coverage
difference is −6.25 percentage points (95% interval −19.35 to +5.41).
The mean first-choice regret difference is −0.0347 men (95% interval
−0.1045 to +0.0053). Intervals use 10,000 whole-game resamples with
seed 20261112. Excluding the one shared-game target leaves 20 primary
and 31 LCB/L pairs, with the same primary zero contrast and a
+0.0013-man LCB/L best-of-three contrast.

The count-and-blockers variant broadens coverage over exact geometry
in this event. The common-target comparison gives little information
about different source rankings because most targets keep their
original source. The results characterize offline Scan candidates.

## Reproduction

Use the [published freeze](FREEZE.md) and [engine digests](provenance.json).
From the DILF root, with the pinned FMJD adapter and Scan binary:

```sh
set -eu
export PYTHONPATH="$PWD:$DRAUGHT_MASTER_ROOT"
study=research/mtdp/jass_benchmark/seventh-pRW2Ahpz
tmpdir=$(mktemp -d)
gzip -dc "$study/score-plan.json.gz" > "$tmpdir/score-plan.json"
for role in source target; do
  case "$role" in source) depth=12 ;; target) depth=16 ;; esac
  python -m research.mtdp.jass_scan_reference --role "$role" --depth "$depth" \
    --manifest "$tmpdir/score-plan.json" --scan "$SCAN_ROOT/scan_linux" \
    --output "$tmpdir/$role.json"
  gzip -dc "$study/$role-depth$depth.json.gz" | cmp - "$tmpdir/$role.json"
done
python -m research.mtdp.jass_benchmark.geometry_ablation analyze \
  --provenance "$study/provenance.json" --source "$tmpdir/source.json" \
  --target "$tmpdir/target.json" --output "$tmpdir/comparison.json"
gzip -dc "$study/comparison.json.gz" | cmp - "$tmpdir/comparison.json"
```

Two scoring runs per role and two analyses reproduced byte for byte.
Source scores agree across 94 shared source-reference instances with
the preceding cohorts. The analysis SHA-256 is
`88946e1cee8e525ade475c3a7352e479b0ae4bfe879b732ee04d0d212a8860b5`.
The historical sixth-cohort analysis reproduced with the new code.
107 MTDP and 643 DILF tests passed. The optional live Jass catalogue
test was skipped because its binary was absent.
