# Jass move ordering on a second independent rapid cohort

The [frozen cohort](README.md) contains 75 quiet target positions from 60
games at the 20 June 2026 Yearly Rapid Arena. Each target has a PC Blues
source with the same signature and identical legal moves. The freeze
was [published before scoring](https://github.com/jfrancoiscollin/dilf/commit/ad2ca47ebfbcd9ab0346d7feafaf156f49653fb5).
Only 0.61% of novel quiet positions with a move choice had such a source.

## Scan 3.1 reference

At successor depth 12, **40/75 pairs had disjoint optimal sets**. All
transferred regrets below are on the ordinary Scan score scale.

| Candidates from PC Blues | Contains a target optimum | Loss ≥ 0.50 men | Maximum loss (men) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First move | 32/75 | 7/75 | 3.80 |
| Best available among the first three | 52/75 | 1/75 | 1.20 |

The median positive loss is 0.06 men for either candidate list.
One list of three contains the full legal set. This diagnostic credits
the best available candidate, whereas the Jass benchmark scores the move
actually chosen under the assigned node budget.

## Jass searches

All 675 searches consumed exactly their assigned budget. The traces
verify the requested root order and complete depth-one coverage.
The three arms used the original benchmark's binaries and settings.

Each quality delta is the Scan regret of Jass's chosen move with transfer
minus that in the control. Negative values favor transfer. Every comparison
includes 75 ordinary-score pairs. For the game-weighted result, 15 FENs
seen in multiple games were each assigned to the first game ID in their
manifest entry, leaving 51 contributing games.

| Nodes | Mean delta vs canonical, per position (men) | Mean delta vs native, per position (men) | Mean delta vs canonical, per game (men) | Mean delta vs native, per game (men) | Scan optima, transfer / canonical / native |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10,000 | −0.0151 | −0.0075 | −0.0248 | −0.0110 | 25 / 21 / 16 |
| 50,000 | +0.0004 | −0.0133 | −0.0010 | −0.0143 | 23 / 24 / 24 |
| 200,000 | +0.0187 | +0.0148 | +0.0160 | +0.0041 | 25 / 30 / 25 |

All median regret deltas are zero. At the deepest completed depth common
to the three arms, median node deltas for transfer versus native are
+1,104, +5,963 and +27,943, and versus canonical are −141, −808 and −2,630.
Native Jass reached greater mean depth at every budget in both tournaments.
The small quality advantage against the canonical control at 50,000 and
200,000 nodes in the [first cohort](../RESULTS_2026-11-12.md) did not
replicate consistently. The 200,000-node mean favors both controls in the
second cohort, including after weighting by game. The observed quality
differences are descriptive and do not establish a playing-strength gain.

## Reproduction

Use the [original pinned build and inputs](../RESULTS_2026-11-12.md#reproduce).
From the DILF root:

```sh
experiment=research/mtdp/jass_benchmark
cohort="$experiment/replication-iPQl7g7q"
output=/tmp/mtdp-jass-replication
mkdir -p "$output"
PYTHONPATH="$PWD:$DRAUGHT_MASTER_ROOT" python -m research.mtdp.jass_scan_reference \
  --manifest "$cohort/cohort-manifest.json" --scan "$SCAN_ROOT/scan_linux" \
  --output "$output/scan-reference.json"
PYTHONPATH="$PWD" python "$experiment/run.py" \
  --manifest "$cohort/cohort-manifest.json" --scan-reference "$output/scan-reference.json" \
  --runner "$JASS_BUILD/mtdp_jass_benchmark" --output-dir "$output"
gzip -dc "$cohort/scan-reference.json.gz" | cmp - "$output/scan-reference.json"
gzip -dc "$cohort/root-order-traces.jsonl.gz" | cmp - "$output/root-order-traces.jsonl"
```

- [Scan scores](scan-reference.json.gz), [paired receipts](paired-results.jsonl.gz)
  and [root traces](root-order-traces.jsonl.gz) contain the complete measurements.
- [Summary](summary.json.gz) reports results per budget, per source game
  and per target game. [Build receipt](jass-build-manifest.json) records hashes.
- Both runs reproduced the Scan scores and root traces byte for byte. The
  paired receipts matched after removing elapsed wall time. The 81 MTDP
  tests, 643 general DILF tests and all 10 CTest tests for Jass passed.
