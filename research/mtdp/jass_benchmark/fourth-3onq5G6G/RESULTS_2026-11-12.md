# Fourth held-out tournament — fixed source ranking, deeper target reference

The [preregistered experiment](../FOURTH_COHORT_CROSS_DEPTH_PROTOCOL_2026-11-12.md)
uses the [cohort published before scoring](https://github.com/jfrancoiscollin/dilf/commit/2eb1c84132723d3913a3b17850f0bdc7552c7f55).
The 74 matched positions in the 28 August 2026 standard 3+0 arena
represent 0.48% of the 15,424 novel, nonterminal quiet-signature FENs.
Scan 3.1 scored 583 successors at source depth 12 and 769 at target
depth 16, one thread with a cold transposition table per evaluation,
without book or tablebases. There were no missing scores or
post-freeze exclusions.

## Signature agreement and the equal-size control

Forty of 74 pairs had disjoint sets of optimal moves across the two
fixed references. The first transferred move covered an optimum on
30/74 targets. Among 73 ordinary-score targets, its mean regret was
0.139 men, six had regret of at least half a man, and the maximum was
1.34 men. These figures compare a depth-12 source to a depth-16 target.

The three candidate moves were selected before examining their target
scores. Transfer ranks by source score with bytewise Jass key ties.
The canonical control uses the first three keys in the same ordering.

| Candidate list | Target optimum covered | Ordinary scores | Mean regret | Median regret | Maximum | Regret ≥0.50 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Transfer | 55/74 | 73 | 0.020 | 0.00 | 0.44 | 0 |
| Canonical | 26/74 | 73 | 0.171 | 0.02 | 1.40 | 8 |

Both covered an optimum in 21 cases. Transfer alone covered 34,
canonical alone covered five, and neither covered 14. The paired
coverage difference was **+39.2 percentage points** (95% interval
+22.2 to +55.4; 56 target-game clusters). The paired mean ordinary
regret difference was **−0.150 men** (95% interval −0.266 to −0.050;
55 clusters), with a paired median of −0.01. Intervals use 10,000
whole-game bootstrap replicates with seed 20261112. Each position
belongs to its first target game ID lexicographically. One target
has a categorical loss under both arms and contributes to coverage
only. No categorical score was subtracted.

The lists were disjoint on 29 targets and shared one, two and three
moves on 26, 18 and one target, respectively. The identical list was
the only target with exactly three legal moves. There were no targets
with fewer than three. Source scores tied at the selection cutoff
on 18/74 targets.

Excluding the nine targets found in several games leaves 65 targets
from 49 owning games. Coverage was 46/65 versus 19/65, a paired gap
of +41.5 points (95% interval +23.2 to +58.5). The ordinary regret
delta was −0.170 men (95% interval −0.295 to −0.058; 64 positions
from 48 games).

The fourth event supports the shortlist signal against the
size-matched canonical control when ranking depth is held fixed.
The mean cost uses the best candidate under the target reference.
A new node-matched Jass benchmark is needed to establish a change in
actual move quality. This event measures a different cadence and
covers a small fraction of eligible quiet positions.

## Reproduction

Start from the [frozen manifest and build instructions](FREEZE.md).
Use the pinned Scan binary, configuration and evaluation file in
[provenance.json](provenance.json). Run from the DILF root.

```sh
set -eu
export PYTHONPATH="$PWD:$DRAUGHT_MASTER_ROOT"
study=research/mtdp/jass_benchmark/fourth-3onq5G6G
tmpdir=$(mktemp -d)
gzip -dc "$study/cohort-manifest.json.gz" > "$tmpdir/manifest.json"
for role in source target; do
  case "$role" in source) depth=12 ;; target) depth=16 ;; esac
  python -m research.mtdp.jass_scan_reference --role "$role" --depth "$depth" \
    --manifest "$tmpdir/manifest.json" --scan "$SCAN_ROOT/scan_linux" \
    --output "$tmpdir/$role.json"
  gzip -dc "$study/$role-depth$depth.json.gz" | cmp - "$tmpdir/$role.json"
done
python -m research.mtdp.jass_benchmark.fourth_cohort_cross_depth \
  --provenance "$study/provenance.json" --source "$tmpdir/source.json" \
  --target "$tmpdir/target.json" --output "$tmpdir/comparison.json"
gzip -dc "$study/comparison.json.gz" | cmp - "$tmpdir/comparison.json"
```

The analysis archive includes both candidate lists for every target,
paired regret difference frequencies, whole-game bootstrap intervals,
and the sensitivity analysis that excludes positions appearing in more
than one game. The archived references contain all legal successor
scores and the comparison records their uncompressed SHA-256 digests.

Two independent source and target scoring runs reproduced their
respective JSON files byte for byte. Source scores agreed exactly
in all 41 instances of shared PC Blues FENs with the earlier
references. The default depth-12 scorer also reproduced the
third-cohort reference with the new role option absent. Two analyses
with distinct Python hash seeds produced identical JSON, SHA-256
`4eb8d924d050a1b1159b3b8969297b11f4bef53c2d6da686c2120dbb5b079fd8`.
All 97 MTDP tests (including the live Jass catalogue check) and 643
DILF tests passed.
