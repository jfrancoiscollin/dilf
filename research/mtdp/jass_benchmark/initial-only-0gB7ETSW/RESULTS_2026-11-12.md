# Third independent event — Jass first-iteration ordering

This run follows the [preregistered protocol](../../JASS_INITIAL_ONLY_PROTOCOL.md)
on the 17 targets from 11 games
[frozen before scoring](https://github.com/jfrancoiscollin/dilf/commit/7e00678710a8090a18879aefbe0ce689856c7f6e).
All source positions are from PC Blues. The 17 targets were the 0.37% of
novel quiet positions with a choice that matched a PC Blues source and
its canonical legal actions.

## Does the signature preserve the best move?

Scan 3.1 scored each legal successor at depth 12 with one thread, cold
TT, no book and no tablebases, on the pinned binary and evaluation file.
The source and target had disjoint sets of optimal moves in **11/17**
pairs, with ties allowed. Cost is measured on Scan's scale in men.

| PC Blues candidates | Target optima covered | Positive costs | Costs ≥ 0.50 | Mean | Median | Maximum |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| First | 6/17 | 11 | 4 | 0.362 | 0.03 | 1.56 |
| Best available of the first three | 13/17 | 4 | 0 | 0.009 | 0.00 | 0.07 |

The worst first-choice transfer was **19-24** (−1.24) with **17-21**
available (+0.32) in the target. All 17 transfers used ordinary scores.
Three moves were a strict subset of the legal set for every target.

## Does seeding Jass help?

Each of 153 searches consumed exactly 10,000, 50,000 or 200,000 nodes as
assigned. For each target and budget, the native order, transferred
first-iteration prefix and canonical first-iteration prefix selected
**the same move**. The transferred and canonical initial orders differed
on all 17 targets. Across 1,875 recorded root attempts, the runner
verified full first-depth coverage and native adaptive ordering from
depth two, including the visited prefixes of incomplete attempts. The
ordered arms applied their schedules once per search, the native arm
zero times. Each search started with a fresh 1 MiB TT.

| Nodes | Targets with a Scan-optimal Jass choice, each arm | Mean completed depth, transfer / canonical / native |
| ---: | ---: | --- |
| 10,000 | 7/17 | 8.00 / 7.94 / 7.94 |
| 50,000 | 8/17 | 11.24 / 11.24 / 11.24 |
| 200,000 | 6/17 | 14.59 / 14.59 / 14.53 |

At 200,000 nodes, the transfer-minus-canonical regret delta had zero
mean and median per position and per target game. All 17 comparisons
had ordinary scores. The 95% target-game bootstrap interval was [0, 0]
(10,000 resamples, seed 20261112; FENs assigned to the first target game
in lexicographic order). Leaving out each selected source game in turn
also gave zero. The interval reflects identical choices in this small
sample and provides limited evidence about other tournaments. The
median node delta at the last common completed depth was zero against
canonical and +3 nodes against native at each budget. The gain in
availability of a Scan-optimal move among the three candidates did not
improve Jass's selected move in this cohort.

## Reproduction

Use the frozen [cohort and input hashes](README.md), pinned
[Scan checkout](../../BEST_MOVE_BASELINE_2026-11-11.md#reproduction), and
the [patched initial-only Jass build](../../jass_preflight/INITIAL_ONLY_RESULTS_2026-11-12.md#reproduction).
Run from the DILF checkout.

```sh
export PYTHONPATH="$PWD:$DRAUGHT_MASTER_ROOT"
cohort=research/mtdp/jass_benchmark/initial-only-0gB7ETSW
gzip -dc "$cohort/cohort-manifest.json.gz" > /tmp/mtdp-third-frozen.json
cmake --build "$JASS_BUILD" --target mtdp_jass_benchmark -j 4
python -m research.mtdp.jass_scan_reference \
  --manifest /tmp/mtdp-third-frozen.json --scan "$SCAN_ROOT/scan_linux" \
  --output /tmp/mtdp-third-reference.json
python -m research.mtdp.jass_benchmark.run --initial-only \
  --manifest /tmp/mtdp-third-frozen.json --scan-reference /tmp/mtdp-third-reference.json \
  --runner "$JASS_BUILD/mtdp_jass_benchmark" --output-dir /tmp/mtdp-third-results
python -m research.mtdp.jass_benchmark.initial_only_analysis \
  --paired-results /tmp/mtdp-third-results/paired-results.jsonl \
  --scan-reference /tmp/mtdp-third-reference.json --output /tmp/mtdp-third-analysis.json
cmp "$cohort/analysis.json" /tmp/mtdp-third-analysis.json
```

Two Scan runs reproduced the same JSON
(`0b2a6306721c367f148108f149dc3c37e7c1d48c69c00752f1d5d79e46f724d9`).
Two benchmark runs reproduced the same uncompressed traces
(`295a3fb7444854c81178a7eb3527e1c27e10922aa6be8fa5a5c45dedc9d8d3ec`)
and receipts apart from wall time. The historical persistent-order
runner reproduced its archived first-cohort traces on the patched build
with the new option absent. All 83 MTDP tests, 643 DILF tests and
`jass_tests` passed.
