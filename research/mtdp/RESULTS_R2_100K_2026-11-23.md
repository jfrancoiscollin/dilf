## Scientific verdict

**PASS: integrity / bounded structural descriptive audit.**
**NOT ADMITTED: strategic equivalence, pruning safety, FMJD legality,
independent population generalization or D1 new cohort.**

The preliminary data show that exact board repeats exist but are *uncommon
within the deterministic pilot*. A dimensionality-reduction hypothesis
based on *near-equivalent* positions remains open; it must be tested against
best-move stability, legal successor constraints and independent Scan/Jass
scoring, not read from a raw deduplication statistic.
